A home for what we're building.

The foundation

TGG HUB is an ecosystem built around independent products, tools, and ideas developed under TGG.

Instead of treating every project as another disconnected creation, TGG HUB provides a shared home where they can be discovered, understood, and eventually connected.

Build things that deserve to exist.

TGG HUB is built around a simple idea: meaningful products should be given room to evolve. Some ideas may become tools. Some may become platforms. Others may become experiments that lead somewhere completely unexpected.

01 / Principles

How we build.

01

Independence

Every project can maintain its own identity, technology, audience, and development path.

02

Utility

Building for the sake of building isn't enough. Projects should create useful, interesting, or meaningful experiences.

03

Evolution

Ideas change. Products improve. The ecosystem is designed to grow with them.

The ecosystem isn't finished.

TGG HUB is intentionally built to leave room for new directions. As projects mature, new products are created, and new ideas are tested, the ecosystem can expand with them.

What exists today is only the beginning.
