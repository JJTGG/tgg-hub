About TGG HUB

One ecosystem.
Independent things.

TGG HUB is the ecosystem layer connecting products, tools, and projects built under TGG — giving each one room to become what it is meant to be.

01 / What is TGG HUB?

More than a collection of projects.

TGG HUB exists to bring independent products and ideas into one broader ecosystem.

The projects connected to TGG HUB are not meant to become copies of one another or pieces of one giant application. Each project has its own identity, purpose, technology, and direction.

TGG HUB provides the layer that connects them.

02 / The Model

Independent by design.

A project does not need to lose its identity simply because it belongs to a larger ecosystem.

Products under TGG HUB can have their own repositories, domains, interfaces, technologies, and development lifecycles.

TGG HUB sits above that structure — connecting the ecosystem without absorbing the products themselves.

03 / Principles

Built around a few simple ideas.

The ecosystem can grow without sacrificing the independence of the things inside it.

Independence

Every product should have the freedom to evolve according to its own purpose.

Utility

Projects should solve real problems, provide meaningful value, or create useful experiences.

Evolution

TGG HUB is designed to grow as new ideas, products, and opportunities emerge.

04 / What's ahead

The ecosystem is only the beginning.

TGG HUB will evolve alongside the products connected to it. As they mature, new connections, experiences, and possibilities can be introduced without changing what makes each project independent.
